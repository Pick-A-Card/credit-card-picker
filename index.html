What your points are worth

Pick what one point is worth to you, in cents. Recommendations multiply each card's earn rate by this value, so points you can only spend in one place (like GM Rewards) don't beat real cash back. Only the rewards programs of cards in your wallet are shown.

Card updates

Card terms and rotating categories are checked every time you open the app. Cards you haven't edited update automatically, and you can undo any change.
