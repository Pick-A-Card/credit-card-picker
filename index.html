At a glance

Recent purchases

Logged purchases count toward spending caps (rotating 5% caps, Custom Cash, etc).

Account

What your points are worth

Cents per point, to you. Recommendations multiply each card's earn rate by this value, so restricted points (like GM Rewards) don't beat real cash back.

Card updates

Card terms and rotating categories are checked every time you open the app. Cards you haven't edited update automatically, and you can undo any change.

Location

Backup

All data stays on this phone (browser storage). Export to move it to another device.

About

Pick-A-Card is free, with no ads and no tracking. If it earns you a little extra cash back, you can buy me a coffee ☕.
