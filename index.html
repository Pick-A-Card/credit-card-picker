Install on your phone

Pick-A-Card runs in your browser, but installing it gives you a home-screen icon and a full-screen app.

📱 iPhone / iPad
  1. Open pick-a-card.github.io/credit-card-picker in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, at the bottom or top of the screen).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.
  4. Tap Add, then open Pick-A-Card from your home screen.
  5. For What card here?, allow location when asked. If you said no before: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites → While Using the App.
🤖 Android
  1. Open pick-a-card.github.io/credit-card-picker in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.
  3. Optional: long-press the app icon and drag the What card here? shortcut onto your home screen for one-tap lookups.

Your cards are saved in the browser you installed from. Sign in below to sync them to your other devices.
